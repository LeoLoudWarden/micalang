## Editorial principles

These three articles are *selections, not full records*. The selection criteria, for readers to judge credibility:

- Removed content (e.g. private information) is marked `[...]`.
- AI replies keep their original meaning; only minimal edits for fluency, no beautifying conclusions after the fact.
- The selection favors **decisions and turning points**: which options were proposed, which were rejected, and why. Routine back-and-forth is not included.
- Rejected options and detours are kept as they were. Half of this project's value lies in "why not that way".
